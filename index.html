Sync between devices Each device (phone, PC, browser) keeps its own local database. Sync moves data between them manually — no account or internet service required.

How to sync two devices:
  1. On Device A: tap "Export sync file" below and send yourself the file (email, cloud drive, USB, AirDrop, etc.)
  2. Open that file on Device B and tap "Sync from file"
  3. Device B now merges in anything new from Device A — nothing on Device B is deleted. Repeat in the other direction to bring Device A up to date too.

Merging matches records by ID and keeps whichever version was edited most recently on either device. Includes transactions, invoices, clients, and receipt photos, so larger files are normal.

Advanced: full restore instead of merge
